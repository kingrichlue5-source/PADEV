ABOUT US

Building Alliances to Mitigate Global Environmental Challenges.

Founded in August 2016, Partners in Development (PADEV) is a leading national institution that builds its intervention on the collective competence of its diverse professionals — delivering community-based natural resource management, forest conservation, and livelihood development across over 100 forest-fringed communities in Liberia.

# PADEV Website: Client Content Handoff Guide

**Prepared for:** Partners in Development (PADEV)

**Purpose:** This is the complete content handoff checklist for the current PADEV website. It mirrors the actual database fields, admin forms, public pages, and files required for a content-complete launch.

**Important:** The site currently contains demonstration content. All names, claims, figures, dates, partner references, images, and documents must be reviewed and formally approved by PADEV before publication. Dates should use `YYYY-MM-DD`; date-times should include the time and timezone, for example `2026-10-31 17:00 (GMT)`.

## 1. What the prototype contains

The prototype is a public-facing PADEV information portal with these main areas:

- Home: organization overview, hero messages, the Program Structure section and its cards, impact figures, all published programmes, featured projects, partner logos, stories, and latest updates.
- About PADEV: mission, background, strategic focus, and organizational information.
- Our Team: founders, board members, and management team profiles.
- Programmes: strategic areas of work, with individual programme detail pages.
- Projects: project tracker with status, county, progress, partners, dates, and results.
- Publications: reports, plans, research, policies, and downloadable documents.
- Procurement: tender and contracting opportunities with deadlines and submission instructions.
- Careers: current vacancies and application information.
- Contact: public contact details and an enquiry form.

The website is content-driven. Once approved text, photographs, logos, reports, and contact details are supplied, they can be entered through the admin panel without redesigning the pages.

## 2. Content needed from PADEV

### A. Organization and brand information

Please confirm the following:

- Official organization name.
- Official acronym and preferred capitalization.
- Final logo in PNG format with a transparent background, if available.
- One-sentence organization description.
- Full mission statement.
- Vision statement.
- Core values.
- Year established and a short organizational history.
- Official email address.
- Official telephone number(s).
- Complete office address.
- Official social media URLs.
- Any required legal, registration, safeguarding, privacy, or donor acknowledgements.

### B. Homepage hero slides

The homepage carousel supports multiple slides. Each slide shows a badge, headline, short description, image, and a **Read More** button that opens the slide's own detail page. For each slide, provide:

- Short badge or category label.
- Main headline.
- Supporting description (one or two sentences shown on the homepage).
- Full article text for the Read More page.
- One strong landscape or activity photograph.

Recommended writing format:

```text
Badge: Conservation in Action
Headline: Protecting Liberia's Forests with Communities
Description: Two or three sentences explaining the work, location, and benefit.
Read More article: The full story in three to five short paragraphs. Start a new paragraph on a new line.
Image: filename.jpg
```

Keep the headline short enough to display clearly on mobile. Use real PADEV photographs wherever possible.

### C. Programmes

Create one content sheet for each programme. The admin form expects:

| Field | Information to provide |
| --- | --- |
| Programme title | Official programme name |
| Short description | Card summary, maximum 500 characters |
| Full description | Purpose, approach, activities, and results |
| Icon suggestion | A simple symbol that represents the programme |
| Status | Active, upcoming, or completed |
| Primary county | County or nationwide coverage |
| Budget | Optional approved figure and currency |
| Target beneficiaries | Specific people, communities, groups, or institutions |
| Start and end dates | Confirmed programme dates |
| Cover image | Clear programme photograph |
| Featured | Priority only — featured programmes are listed first if more than six programmes exist; every programme is shown |

Suggested programme content structure:

1. What the programme addresses.
2. Who benefits.
3. Where the work takes place.
4. Main activities.
5. Results or expected results.
6. Partners and funding sources.
7. Safeguarding, inclusion, or sustainability considerations.

### D. Projects

For every project, provide:

- Official project title.
- Related programme.
- Short summary for project cards, maximum 500 characters.
- Full project description.
- Project photograph.
- Status: planning, ongoing, completed, or on hold.
- County and specific location or communities.
- Percentage complete, from 0 to 100.
- Approved budget, if publishable.
- Contractor, implementing partner, client, or funder.
- Start date and completion date.
- Whether the project should be featured.

Project descriptions should state the problem, intervention, location, partners, beneficiaries, measurable results, and lessons learned. Please verify every number and date before submission.

### E. News and field updates

For each article, provide:

- Headline.
- Category: press release, agency announcement, field progress update, or official event.
- Author or communications unit.
- Short excerpt, maximum 400 characters.
- Full article.
- Publication date.
- Featured image.
- Whether it should appear as a featured update.

Each article should answer: What happened? Where and when? Who participated? Why does it matter? What happens next? Include captions and photo credits where applicable.

### F. Success stories

For each story, provide:

- Story title.
- Beneficiary name, community, group, or institution.
- County.
- A verified quotation, maximum 300 characters.
- Full background and impact story.
- Featured photograph.
- Consent confirmation for the person or community shown.
- Whether it should appear on the homepage.

Stories should show a clear change before and after the intervention without exaggerating results. Obtain permission before publishing names, faces, personal details, or quotations.

### G. Team, founders, and board

For every person to be published, provide:

- Full name.
- Current position.
- Category: management team, founders, or board of directors.
- Short professional biography.
- High-quality headshot.
- Public work email, only if the person approves it.
- Display order.
- Confirmation that the profile is active.

Biographies should normally be one to three short paragraphs and should focus on current responsibilities, relevant experience, and contribution to PADEV.

### H. Clients and partners

For each organization, provide:

- Official organization name.
- Approved logo.
- Official website URL.
- Display order.
- Confirmation that the logo may be displayed publicly.

Please provide logos as SVG, PNG, or high-resolution JPG files and confirm any donor brand guidelines.

### I. Publications and reports

For every document, provide:

- Official title.
- Category: annual report, strategic plan, policy/framework, procurement or audit report, research, or case study.
- Short description, maximum 500 characters.
- Final PDF file.
- Optional cover image.
- Publication date.
- Public file size, if required.
- Confirmation that the file is approved for public download.

Use final, accessible PDFs with a clear title page and consistent file names such as `PADEV_Annual_Report_2025.pdf`.

### J. Procurement opportunities

For each opportunity, provide:

- Tender title.
- Unique reference number.
- Category: RFQ, RFP, EOI, or national competitive bidding.
- Scope of work and submission requirements.
- Tender document in PDF format.
- Opening date.
- Closing date and exact time.
- Status: open, under evaluation, awarded, or closed.
- Procurement contact email.
- Submission location or submission method.

Procurement content should be reviewed by the authorized procurement officer before publication. Dates and times must include the correct time zone where relevant.

### K. Career vacancies

For each vacancy, provide:

- Job title.
- Vacancy reference number.
- Department.
- Duty station or location.
- Employment type: full-time, contractual, consultancy, or internship.
- Responsibilities and role summary.
- Qualifications, experience, and skills.
- Application deadline.
- Application email or method.
- Confirmation that the vacancy is active.

Remove vacancies promptly after the deadline or mark them inactive in the admin panel.

## 3. Admin panel content areas

The administration panel contains these manageable sections:

1. **Site Settings:** name, acronym, logo, contact details, address, and social links. This is a single organization-wide record.
2. **Navigation Menus:** menu labels, URLs, ordering, badges, and child menu items.
3. **Hero Slides:** homepage carousel content, images, the full article behind each Read More button, order, and active state.
4. **Program Structure:** the homepage cards that float below the hero — title, short copy, full detail article, icon, colour theme, order, and active state. The section heading ("Program Structure") and its introduction are fixed text in the page template, shown above the cards on the dark hero background; the cards appear only when at least one is active.
5. **Development Programmes:** programme descriptions, locations, dates, budgets, beneficiaries, and featured state.
6. **County Projects:** project details, status, progress, dates, partners, and featured state.
7. **News and Updates:** articles, categories, authors, dates, images, and featured state.
8. **Success Stories:** beneficiary stories, quotes, locations, images, and featured state.
9. **Team Members:** people, roles, biographies, photos, order, and active state.
10. **Clients and Partners:** organization names, logos, websites, order, and active state.
11. **Publications and Reports:** downloadable PDF records and publication metadata.
12. **Procurement Opportunities:** tenders, reference numbers, documents, deadlines, and status.
13. **Career Vacancies:** job descriptions, requirements, deadlines, and status.
14. **Contact Submissions:** enquiries received from the public. These are read-only records; staff can mark them as read.

## 4. Media submission checklist

- Use original PADEV photographs or images for which PADEV has permission.
- Name files clearly: `project-name-location-year.jpg`.
- Avoid blurry, heavily compressed, dark, or duplicated images.
- Provide a short caption and photo credit for every public photograph.
- Confirm consent for identifiable people, especially children and vulnerable groups.
- Provide logos with transparent backgrounds where possible.
- Check that PDFs open correctly and do not contain confidential information.
- Keep a separate archive of the original files and signed consent records.

## 5. Editorial and approval checklist

Before content is entered into the website, PADEV should confirm:

- Names, titles, dates, figures, counties, and partner names are correct.
- The content has been approved by the responsible programme or communications lead.
- Funding and donor references follow the relevant brand requirements.
- Claims and impact figures can be supported by internal records.
- Personal information and photographs have the required consent.
- Procurement and vacancy deadlines are current.
- The correct contact email is listed for each area.
- The content is written in clear, consistent English.

## 7. How to share the prototype

The development copy can be demonstrated locally by starting the Django server and opening:

```text
http://127.0.0.1:8000/
```

That address works only on the developer's computer. For the client to access it remotely, deploy the prototype to a staging server or use an approved temporary preview service. Share the public staging URL, not the local address. The admin URL should be shared separately and only with authorized PADEV staff.

## 10. Exact CMS field checklist

This section is aligned with the current Django models and should be used as the source of truth when preparing the spreadsheet.

### Site settings (one record only)

Required: `site_name`, `agency_acronym`, `contact_email`, `contact_phone`, and `address`.

Optional: `logo`, `social_links`, and `hero_background_image`. Social links should be supplied as label and URL pairs using labels such as `facebook`, `instagram`, `twitter`, `linkedin`, or `youtube`.

### Homepage hero slides

Required: `title`, display `order`, and `is_active`.

Optional: badge text, description, slide image, `slug`, and `detail_content` (the full article opened by the Read More button; new paragraphs start on a new line). Button labels and destinations are fixed: every slide has one Read More button that opens that slide's own detail page, so no URL is required.

### Program structure cards

Required: `title`, `description` (maximum 300 characters), and `is_active`.

Optional: `detail_content` (the full article opened when the title is clicked; new paragraphs start on a new line), `icon_class` (Remix icon such as `ri-tree-line`), `theme` (one of `green`, `gold`, `navy`, `rose`), `slug`, and `order`. Cards are numbered `01`, `02`, … automatically by display order. The grid shows up to four cards per row and hides itself when no card is active. The card title is a link; when `detail_content` is empty the page repeats the short description instead.

### Programmes

Required: title, short description (maximum 500 characters), full description, status, county, and featured decision.

Optional: icon class, cover image, budget, target beneficiaries, start date, end date, and SEO meta description (maximum 160 characters).

Allowed statuses: `Active`, `Upcoming`, `Completed`. Allowed counties are listed in Section 15.

### Projects

Required: title, short description (maximum 500 characters), full description, status, county, progress percentage, and featured decision.

Optional: related programme, cover image, location details, budget, contractor, start date, completion date, and SEO meta description (maximum 160 characters). Progress must be a whole number from `0` to `100`.

Allowed statuses: `Planning`, `Ongoing / In Progress`, `Completed`, `On Hold`.

### News and updates

Required: title, category, excerpt (maximum 400 characters), and full article content.

Optional: author, featured image, featured decision, and SEO meta description (maximum 160 characters). The current database generates `published_date` when a record is first created, so provide the approved date to the administrator for verification or adjustment.

Allowed categories: `Press Release`, `Agency Announcement`, `Field Progress Update`, `Official Event`.

### Success stories

Required: title, beneficiary/community name, county, quote (maximum 300 characters), and full story.

Optional: featured image, featured decision, and SEO meta description (maximum 160 characters). Provide a separate consent reference for every identifiable person or group shown.

### Team members

Required: full name and position. Role category must be `Management Team`, `Founders & Board`, or `Board of Directors`.

Optional: biography, photo, public email, display order, and active decision. Only publish a personal email with the person’s approval.

### Clients and partners

Required: organization name.

Optional in the database: logo, official website, display order, and active decision. For a polished public partner strip, also provide confirmation that each logo may be displayed publicly.

### Publications and reports

Required: title, category, final PDF file, and publication date.

Optional: description (maximum 500 characters), cover image, displayed file size, and SEO meta description. Download count is system-managed and must not be supplied.

Allowed categories: `Annual Report`, `Strategic Plan`, `Policy & Framework`, `Procurement & Financial Audit`, `Research & Case Study`.

### Procurement opportunities

Required: title, unique reference number, category, description, tender PDF, opening date, closing date and time, status, contact email, and submission location.

Optional: SEO meta description (maximum 160 characters). Allowed categories: `RFQ`, `RFP`, `EOI`, `National Competitive Bidding`. Allowed statuses: `Open for Submission`, `Under Evaluation`, `Awarded`, `Closed`.

### Career vacancies

Required: job title, department, location, employment type, role description, requirements, application deadline, contact email, and active decision.

Optional: reference number and SEO meta description (maximum 160 characters). Allowed employment types: `Full-Time`, `Contractual`, `Consultancy`, `Internship`.

### Navigation menu items

Required: visible title, destination URL, display order, and active decision. Optional: parent menu item and badge text. Suggested launch links are Home `/`, About Us `/#about`, Our Team `/team/`, Programmes `/programmes/`, Projects `/projects/`, Publications `/publications/`, Procurement `/procurement/`, Careers `/careers/`, and Contact Us `/contact/`.

### Contact submissions

Visitors create these records; PADEV does not need to prepare them. The form requires full name, email, and message, and accepts phone and subject optionally. Nominate an administrator to monitor submissions and mark them as read. The current form stores submissions in the database but does not automatically send an email notification.

## 11. Fixed content not currently editable in the CMS

Provide final approved copy, or confirm that the current wording may remain, for:

- Homepage hero headline, introductory paragraph, and CTA labels.
- About section heading, organization summary, highlights, and founding-year statement.
- Homepage impact figures: projects delivered, forest-fringed communities, beehives established, and counties reached. The current view uses the project count plus fixed values of `100`, `190`, and `15`; approve the exact figures and the `+` suffix.
- Homepage introductions for programmes, projects, procurement, news, and contact.
- Footer organization description and privacy, safeguarding, copyright, or legal links.
- Contact page instructions, office details, and map/location information.

These items must be supplied in a fixed-copy sheet because changing them requires a template or view update rather than an admin upload.
